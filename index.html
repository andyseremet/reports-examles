Property Location Check

Thinking of buying a house or land in the UK? Check flooding, radon, coal mining, noise, air quality, old landfill, planning restrictions and more, free, using official government and open data.

Use A+ for larger text. The page follows your device's dark mode. To add it to your home screen: on Android, use Install app (or the browser menu); on iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen.

Step 1 of 3

Find the property

Type a postcode, address or place in the box, or tap the map to drop a pin.

On a phone, scroll the page with one finger and move the map with two. On a computer, hold Ctrl (⌘ on Mac) while scrolling to zoom the map. The full-screen button makes the map fill the screen, where one finger moves it.

The layers button changes the background map and shows road noise and the map key. The location button puts the pin where you are.

    Step 2 of 3

    Is the pin on the right property?

    A postcode covers several homes. If the pin is not on the right building, drag it or tap the exact spot.

The postcodes within 500 m go to HM Land Registry, for recent sale prices.
