Property Location Check

Free checks for anyone buying a house or land in England: flooding, radon, coal mining, noise, air quality, landfill, planning restrictions and more, using official government and open data.

1. Choose the location

Or click the map, or drag the pin, to the exact house or plot.

Property Location Report

Choose a location and press “Run the checks”.

The report will list each check with a plain-English result, what it means for a buyer, and what to do next.

How to read the results

  • Look into this Something was found that could affect value, insurance, health or safety. Investigate before you commit.
  • Worth checking Something was found nearby or at a moderate level. Ask questions or order a specialist search.
  • No issue found Nothing was found in the data checked. This is not a guarantee.
  • For information Useful local facts, for example planning restrictions.
  • Check yourself No free live data is available. A link to the official checker is given.
