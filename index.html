Property Location Check

Thinking of buying a house or land in England? Check flooding, radon, coal mining, noise, air quality, old landfill, planning restrictions and more, free, using official data.

Step 1 of 2

Find the property

Use the search 🔍 at the top left of the map to find a postcode, address or place, or tap the map to drop a pin. The layers button at the top right shows road noise and the map key.

    Step 2 of 2

    Is the pin on the right property?
    If not, drag the pin, or click the exact spot on the map.

    Checking official data…

      What you will get

      A report for the exact spot you choose, grouped into Flooding, Ground and land, Living environment and Planning and surroundings. It ends with a checklist of questions for the seller and searches to ask your conveyancer for.

      How to read the results

      • Look into this Something that could affect value, insurance, health or safety. Investigate before you commit.
      • Worth checking Something nearby or at a moderate level. Ask questions or order a specialist search.
      • No issue found Nothing found in the data checked. This is not a guarantee.
      • For information Useful local facts, for example planning restrictions.
